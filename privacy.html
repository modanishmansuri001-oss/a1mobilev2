Privacy Policy

A1 Rewards — Privacy and Data Protection

A1 Rewards mein aapki privacy important hai. Is policy mein bataya gaya hai ki app ya website use karte waqt aapki information kaise collect, use aur protect ki ja sakti hai.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Naam aur mobile number, agar aap account banate hain.
  • OTP verification status aur account-related details.
  • Referral, lead, QR scan, shop visit aur reward activity.
  • Points balance, withdrawal requests aur transaction history.
  • A1 Mobile se hui eligible purchases aur EMI-related records.
  • Support requests aur aapke dwara voluntarily di gayi information.
  • Technical information, jaise device ya app diagnostics, agar website ya app ise collect karti hai.

Kaunsi information actually collect hoti hai, yeh app ke implemented features aur enabled services par depend karega.

2. How We Use Information

  • Account banane aur user verification ke liye.
  • Eligible referrals, leads aur rewards verify karne ke liye.
  • Points balance aur withdrawal requests manage karne ke liye.
  • Customer ke relevant purchase aur EMI records dikhane ke liye.
  • Fraud, duplicate activity aur misuse ki jaanch ke liye.
  • Customer support aur service improvements ke liye.
  • Applicable legal obligations ko poora karne ke liye.

3. OTP and Mobile Number

Mobile OTP ka use account ya eligible activity verify karne ke liye ho sakta hai. Apna OTP kisi ke saath share na karein. Verification service ke provider ke rules bhi apply ho sakte hain.

4. Leads and Referrals

Lead ya referral submit karte waqt sirf wahi information dein jise share karne ki aapko permission ho. Kisi doosre vyakti ka mobile number ya personal data bina uchit permission submit na karein. Verification aur reward eligibility ke liye submitted details process ki ja sakti hain.

5. QR Scans and Instagram Activity

QR scan, campaign participation aur Instagram earning ke dauran submitted screenshots ya activity details ko eligibility verify karne aur rewards manage karne ke liye use kiya ja sakta hai. Screenshot mein unnecessary personal information share na karein.

6. Purchase and EMI Information

Agar aapne A1 Mobile se phone kharida hai, to applicable purchase, installment, payment aur pending amount ke records account mein dikhaye ja sakte hain. Ye records A1 ke internal records par based ho sakte hain. Ye official credit bureau report nahi hain.

7. Points and Withdrawals

Points balance, earning activity aur withdrawal requests ko process karne ke liye related transaction information use ki ja sakti hai. Withdrawal verification ke liye zaroori details process ki ja sakti hain. Sensitive payment information sirf zaroorat aur applicable security requirements ke mutabik handle ki jaani chahiye.

8. Sharing of Information

Personal information ko bina valid reason ke sell nahi kiya jayega. Information zaroorat ke mutabik service providers, verification partners, authorized personnel ya lawful authorities ke saath share ki ja sakti hai, jab service chalane, security ya legal compliance ke liye zaroori ho.

9. Data Security

Information ko unauthorized access, misuse ya loss se bachane ke liye reasonable security measures use karne ka prayas kiya jayega. Internet par koi bhi system 100% security guarantee nahi de sakta. Apna password aur OTP safe rakhein.

10. Data Retention

Information utni der tak rakhi ja sakti hai jitni der account, rewards, customer service, dispute resolution ya legal obligations ke liye zaroori ho. Zaroorat khatam hone par applicable law aur retention requirements ke mutabik data delete ya anonymize kiya ja sakta hai.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Applicable law ke mutabik aap apni personal information ko access, correct ya delete karne ki request kar sakte hain. Kuch records ko legal, financial ya dispute-related reasons se retain karna zaroori ho sakta hai.

12. Account Deletion

Account delete karne ke liye A1 Rewards ke available account settings ya official support channel ka use karein. Agar app mein deletion option available nahi hai, to support se request karein. Deletion process, retention aur kisi bhi pending transaction ka handling applicable law aur service requirements ke mutabik hoga.

13. Children’s Privacy

A1 Rewards un users ke liye nahi hai jo applicable law ke mutabik service use karne ki minimum age ko poora nahi karte. Agar kisi underage user ki information galti se collect ho, to support se contact karein.

14. Policy Updates

Features, services ya legal requirements badalne par is policy ko update kiya ja sakta hai. Updated version isi page par publish kiya jayega. Important changes ke liye, jahan zaroori ho, notice diya jayega.

15. Contact and Privacy Requests

Privacy, account information, correction ya deletion se judi request ke liye A1 Mobile ke official support channel ya shop se sampark karein.

Note: Is page par apna actual support email ya business contact publish karna zaroori hai, taaki users privacy requests bhej saken.
